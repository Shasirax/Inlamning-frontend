Sausage-Basil Ragù over Potato Gnocchi with Parmesan

  1. Preheat a large pot over medium heat.

  2. Meanwhile, peel and small dice onion.

    • 1 medium yellow onion

  3. Once the pot is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add onion and cook, stirring often, until it begins to soften, about 4 minutes.

    • 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil

  4. While the onion is cooking, fill another large pot about halfway with hot water (from the tap); cover and bring to a boil over high heat.

  5. Use the tip of a knife to slice down the length of each sausage. Peel off the casings and discard. Add sausage meat to the pot with the onions and, breaking apart with a spoon, cook until starting to brown, about 5 minutes.

    • 4 pork sausages

  6. Peel and mince garlic.

    • 2 cloves garlic

  7. Add garlic, tomato sauce, water, sugar, and spices to the pot with the sausage and stir to combine. Cook, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened, about 5 minutes.

    • 2 (213 ml) cans tomato sauce
    • ⅔ cup water
    • 1 tsp brown sugar
    • ½ tsp rosemary, dried
    • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
    • ½ tsp salt
    • ¼ tsp black pepper
    • ⅛ tsp cinnamon, ground

  8. Once the water in the other pot is boiling, add gnocchi and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until gnocchi begins to float to the surface, 3-4 minutes. Drain in a colander and set aside.

    • 2 (454 g) pkgs potato gnocchi
    • 1 tsp salt

  9. Meanwhile, finely grate Parmesan and set aside.

    • 28 g Parmesan cheese

  10. Wash and dry basil. Pick leaves off the stems, roll up crosswise, and thinly slice into ribbons. Add to the ragú and stir to combine. Remove from heat.

    • 1 small pkg fresh basil

  11. To serve, divide gnocchi and ragú between plates or bowls. Garnish with Parmesan and enjoy!
