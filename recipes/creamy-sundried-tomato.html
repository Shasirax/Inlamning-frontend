Creamy Sundried Tomato & Spinach Linguine Pasta

  1. Fill a large pot halfway with water, cover, and bring to a boil. Uncover, add salt and pasta, and stir for a few seconds. Cook until desired firmness, 8-10 minutes.

    • about 14 cups water
    • 2 tbsp salt
    • 340 g (1 quarter coin = 57 g) linguine pasta

  2. Heat a sauté pan over medium heat.

  3. Peel and small dice onion. Peel and mince garlic. Thinly slice sun-dried tomatoes.

    • 1 medium yellow onion
    • 4 cloves garlic
    • 12 sun-dried tomatoes, oil-packed

  4. Coat bottom of pan with oil. Add onion, garlic, tomatoes, and crushed red pepper. Stirring frequently, cook until onion is softened, 3-4 minutes.

    • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
    • ¼ tsp crushed red pepper

  5. Wash and dry spinach.

    • 1 (142 g) pkg baby spinach

  6. Add tomato sauce, basil, salt, and pepper to pan. Stir to combine and simmer for 3-4 minutes.

    • 2 (213 ml) cans tomato sauce
    • ½ tsp basil
    • ½ tsp salt
    • ¼ tsp black pepper

  7. Finely grate ¼ cup of Parmesan.

    • 28 g Parmesan cheese

  8. Add yogurt to pan, reduce heat to medium-low, and stir to combine.

    • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt

  9. Add spinach and fold in until wilted. Remove pan from heat until pasta is ready.

  10. Drain pasta in a colander, reserving about 1 cup of the pasta water.

  11. Add pasta and ½ of the water to sauce; toss to coat. If sauce is too thick, add more pasta water to thin it out.

  12. Place pasta in a bowl or on a plate and garnish with grated Parmesan.
